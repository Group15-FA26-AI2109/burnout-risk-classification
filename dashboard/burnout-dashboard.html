Descriptive overview

Five-model held-out comparison

Default threshold 0.5. AP intervals: 2,000 ordinary paired test-row bootstrap resamples, conditional on fitted models. MLP is an unweighted reference configuration.

Signed-grouped SHAP

Sum signed dummy contributions within each observation, then take absolute values and average. Log-odds units; 2,000 test records; 100 effective background records from 1,000 training candidates.

Category attribution

Shares of original-feature attribution magnitude, not explained variance or causal effects. Permutation AP drops are not additive.

Feature ablation and treatment sensitivity

RQ3 is F1 to F2 only. F3 also removes demographics and treatment. F4/F5 are post hoc treatment-removal sensitivity analyses on the existing test set.
